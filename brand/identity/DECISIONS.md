# Brand identity working record

Date: 2026-10-02. Scope: new English product name and logo, plus web-use exports. Existing application is unchanged.

## Brief — ready

Confirmed: current verlark offers English writing practice/review, speaking scenarios, pronunciation feedback, AI coach conversations and progress views. Landing copy targets IELTS, TOEFL and academic English. Product is not launched. User intends a new project with a different stack and has requested a new name and logo. User selected an English primary name for international use.

Bounded assumptions: adult or older student learners; an English brand readable by Chinese-speaking learners; website navigation, browser favicon and repository presentation are primary uses. Broader everyday English remains a plausible extension, not a confirmed roadmap. We are not claiming product superiority or score improvements.

Positioning intention: a focused place to practise written and spoken English, receive useful feedback, and try again with growing confidence.

## Strategy — ready

| Trait | Naming implication | Visible implication | Anti-trait |
|---|---|---|---|
| Encouraging | Inviting sound; room to attempt and retry | Open counterforms, human-scale curves | Infantilising, reward-game imagery |
| Articulate | Readable, pronounceable English spelling | Controlled spacing, legible lettering | Clever spelling at the cost of speech |
| Composed | Name can live beyond an AI trend | Compact silhouette and restrained palette | Flashy or gadget-like |
| Forward moving | Suggest practice becoming expression | Directional relationship without a stock growth arrow | Aggressive performance pressure |

Avoid a name tied only to essay marking, one exam, or a particular model. Avoid a generic robot, brain, sparkle or microphone as the sole identity. Brand personality: calm, constructive and quietly confident; a good practice partner for adult learners.

## Exploration plan

Names are explored in three territories: a place to rehearse; confidence in one's expression; language taking shape through practice. Shortlist decisions and searches will be recorded before visual work. The user delegated naming/logo creation; routine candidate selection will be made with reasons and remains a proposal for adoption. Domain purchase, legal trademark clearance and application rebranding are outside scope.

## Naming — selected proposal: Verlark

Territories: T1 rehearsal space (Opentake, Tryroom); T2 owning expression (Saystead, Ownsay, Saymorrow); T3 expression taking shape / gaining rhythm (Wordward, Draftwing, Linekind, Phravo, Liltide, Verlark). Internal quality retirements: Second Take is too familiar and film-led; Outswell has a swelling association; Rifflet is strongly music-led and has an existing music-service history. These are judgments, not legal conclusions.

Final two-name shortlist is intentionally not padded with conflicted candidates. Recommendation remains based on the name's compact, composed sound and visual rhythm, not on sparse search alone. Canonical spelling: verlark. Suggested pronunciation: VER-lark, /ˈvɜːr.lɑːrk/ (brand guidance, not a dictionary claim). No official Chinese name. Project name, logo wordmark, and prose use lowercase `verlark`. Never shorten the brand to Lark.

Design intent for the coinage is verbal expression with a light, exploratory character; it is not an asserted historical word or an assertion about bird behaviour. Descriptive line: English practice. Proposed line: Find your words. Make them yours.

## Visual direction — ready for sketches

Adult, encouraging, deliberate. An open, asymmetric gesture; strong silhouette and one recognisable interruption rather than multiple tiny details. Typography: substantial lowercase humanist/geometric sans, open counters, restrained spacing. Provisional colour intent: warm red-orange with near-black and paper white; actual palette waits for monochrome review. Avoid blue-green flight ribbons resembling Lark, literal microphone icons, cute owl faces, stock book-plus-chat combinations, and gradients.

V01: Unfolded voice. A compact V-like pair of unequal curved planes, with a distinct open upper turn; the unfolding relationship suggests an attempt becoming an expression. Cost: an airline/checkmark reading if too sharp or symmetric.

V02: Perched phrase. A restrained side-profile bird perched on a short horizontal stroke, with a compact neck/beak relationship. Writing line + voice association. Cost: stronger bird-category / education-mascot association; must remain adult and avoid incidental anatomy.

V03: Wordmark-led. Custom lowercase verlark with a recognisable v/r relationship and terminal rhythm; no independent pictorial symbol. Cost: less ownable at favicon size, but potentially strongest restraint.

Next: inspect separate monochrome sketches, select with visible reasons, perform preliminary visual screening, construct vectors, render and test delivered files. Existing app favicon and product UI remain unchanged.

## Visual decisions and delivery — complete as a design proposal

- Generated V01/V02/V03 individually with the built-in imagegen tool; exact prompts are in `generation-prompts.json`, original outputs in `exploration/`. Actual saved V01/V02 files are 1254 × 1254, V03 is 2073 × 758. These are exploration outputs, not production vectors.
- Inspected all three on a white comparison board. Selected V01: clearer compact silhouette than the perched bird; carries a standalone mark where the wordmark-only approach loses recognition at favicon scale. Its cost is the familiar V / growth-curve family, acknowledged in screening.
- V01.1 vector correction: enlarged the almost closed lower seam into a real gap; replaced raster edge ambiguity with two closed Bézier paths; squared the upper cut and regularised the outward curve. The image's short-left / tall-right relationship remains. The wordmark uses manually positioned Manrope 650, rather than reproducing incidental generated letter distortions.
- Monochrome reviewed on white and reversed on ink before choosing colour. Colour: Ember #CF432D, Ink #242925, Paper #F7F4ED, Apricot #F1CEAE. Colour is not required to distinguish the two mark planes.
- Icon adaptation scales the symbol to 92% inside the square, translates it for optical balance and provides an intentional opaque Ember background. No OS corner mask is baked into the file. It is a web icon/export, not a complete App Store submission package.
- Actual studies: symbol/icon at 1024, 256, 64, 32 and 16 px; full horizontal logo at those total widths. Full logo becomes unsuitable in 16/32/64 px slots; recommended minimum is 176 px wide, standalone wordmark 120 px, symbol/icon 16 px. Tiny uses should use the icon, not force the wordmark into them. The 16 px symbol retains separation; no separate simplified geometry was needed in this inspection.
- Browser inspection confirmed the embedded Manrope font loads. Text-source versus outline comparison at 1360 × 460: mean absolute grayscale difference 0.01659/255; one of 625600 pixels differed by more than 64. Visual comparison showed matching glyph shapes/positions, with rasterisation-level residuals.
- Core SVGs contain actual paths, no raster image embedding; PNGs include transparent logo backgrounds and intentionally opaque icon backgrounds. Exact file checks and bounds: `../verlark/previews/verification.json`.
- Representative brand use: `../verlark/previews/brand-board.png`, including a web navigation/practice page study. This is a brand application preview, not an implemented product redesign.
- Limitations: [SCREENING.md](SCREENING.md). No purchased domain, trademark clearance, audience testing or app-source branding changes. Proposed identity delivered with editable sources, exports, previews and usage guidance.
